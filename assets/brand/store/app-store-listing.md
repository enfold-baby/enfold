## Description [4000]
Enfold is a calm baby care log for parents and caregivers. Keep feeds, sleep, diapers, medicine, pumping, tummy time and growth in one gentle place, then find clear, reassuring guidance when you wonder "is this normal?".

Built for 3am
Big targets and quiet screens keep tired moments short. The logs you make most are two taps away, and everything works offline, because hospital and nursery signal is not always there.

Everything in one place
- Feeds: breast (with side), pumped milk, formula or bottle, with amounts and duration
- Sleep: log a nap after the fact, or start a sleep and tap Wake up later
- Time awake: see how long baby has been awake since the last sleep
- Diapers: wet, dirty, or both
- Medicine and vitamins, with gentle daily reminders
- Pumping and tummy time
- Growth: weight, length and head, in metric or imperial
- Milestones as they happen
- Pregnancy: due date, kick counts and appointment notes

Care is shared
Create a free account to sync between phones and invite your partner or a caregiver. Everyone sees the same day, and each log shows who added it, without turning care into scorekeeping. An account is optional: Enfold works fully on your phone without one.

Reassurance before charts
Learn topics start with what is common, what to watch, and when to call a professional. Share a 7-day summary as a PDF before a doctor or nurse visit.

Kind, never clinical
No streaks, no guilt, no scores for missed logs or messy days. A small snapshot of today, never a grade.

Your family data stays family data
No ads and no ad or analytics SDKs. We never sell or share your data. Without an account your logs stay on your phone. With one, they sync over an encrypted connection, and you can delete your account and data from Settings at any time.

Free for every parent. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and safety guidance never sits behind a paywall.

Enfold is a log and an educational companion. It does not diagnose or replace professional medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
