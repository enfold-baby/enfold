## Review status

Each card carries a `reviewer` block. `"Pending physician review"` means the text was
written by the team from published guidance (AAP, NHS, WHO) and has not yet been signed off
by a clinician. Reviewed cards name the reviewer, credentials and date. The workflow is in
`feature/todos/CONTENT_REVIEW.md`. Corrections are welcome as issues; clinicians who want
to review cards can write to support@enfold.baby.

None of this is medical advice. See the disclaimer inside every card.
