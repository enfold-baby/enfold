# Security policy

Enfold stores feeding, sleep and health notes about babies. We take reports seriously and
answer fast.

## Reporting

Email **support@enfold.baby**. Include steps to reproduce and, if it matters, the app
version and platform. Please do not open a public issue and please do not test against
real accounts on `api.enfold.baby` beyond what is needed to demonstrate the problem.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 days and a fix or a plan within 14 days for
anything that touches account access or family data. We will credit you in the release
notes unless you prefer not to be named.

## Scope

- The Flutter app (`lib/`, `android/`, `ios/`)
- The API (`backend/`) as deployed at `api.enfold.baby`
- The website (`landing/`) at `enfold.baby`

Out of scope: rate-limit findings on the marketing site, findings that need a rooted or
jailbroken device, and reports generated by automated scanners without a working proof.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on Google Play and the App Store receives fixes. The API is
deployed from `main`.
