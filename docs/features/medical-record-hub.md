# Medical Record Hub

## Goal

Keep baby health records organized for parents and easier to summarize during pediatric visits.

## Planned Records

- Growth: weight, length, head circumference.
- Vaccines: type, date, batch/notes when provided.
- Appointments: date, clinician, notes, follow-up.
- Documents: discharge notes, lab PDFs/images, prescriptions.

## MVP Boundary

Growth measurements, history trend charts, milestones, and 7-day visit PDF are **shipped**. Vaccines, document attachments, WHO percentile curves, and a full record hub are still later.
