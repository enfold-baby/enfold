# Code of conduct

Enfold is made for tired parents by tired parents. Be kind.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful, assume good faith, no harassment, no gatekeeping, no medical
shaming of parents' choices (breast, bottle, co-sleeping debates included).

Report problems to support@enfold.baby. Maintainers will act on reports privately and
proportionately, from a quiet word to removal from the project.
