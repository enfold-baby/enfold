§ 01

The code is public

On 17 September 2026 we published all of Enfold on GitHub: github.com/enfold-baby/enfold. A baby app holds your family’s most private notes. “No ads, no selling data” is easy to promise and hard to prove, so we made it checkable.

  • What’s open: the Flutter app for iOS and Android, the FastAPI backend behind api.enfold.baby, and this website, all under the AGPL-3.0 with an app store covenant.
  • Learn cards: readable by anyone under CC BY-NC-ND, with the reviewing clinician’s name and date on each. Cards not yet reviewed say so.
  • What stays ours: the Enfold name, logo and illustrations (trademark policy), so a fork can’t ship as “Enfold” with ads.
  • Contributing: translations first (Romanian next), bugs with steps, accessibility. No CLA, you keep your copyright. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
  • Security: found something? Email support@enfold.baby or use GitHub’s private reporting. See SECURITY.md.
  • Funding: donations only, via GitHub Sponsors or the supporters wall. Nothing is paywalled in the app.

§ 02

Why we run as an open startup

Enfold exists because parents deserve calm tools, and because we’ve walked hard roads to get here. Being open means we tell you what we’re building, what we won’t do (ads, selling family data), and what’s still unfinished. You deserve that honesty.

§ 03

Promises we keep in public

  • No ads and no ad SDKs in the app.
  • No selling family data for targeting.
  • Privacy-first analytics on the website (Simple Analytics, cookieless).
  • Educational, not medical advice: clinicians help us; the app doesn’t diagnose.
  • Self-funded for now: we take help (e.g. builder credits) without selling out parents.
  • Open source: the code stays public under the AGPL, so the promises above can be checked, not just trusted.

§ 05

How we build

  • Mobile: Flutter (one codebase for Android & iOS)
  • Offline-first: local SQLite/Drift + sync when signed in
  • API: FastAPI on our VPS · api.enfold.baby
  • Landing: static site · this page
  • Auth: email magic codes (no passwords)
  • Code: GitHub, AGPL-3.0, CI on every push
