§ 01

Why we run as an open startup

BloomDue exists because parents deserve calm tools — and because we’ve walked hard roads to get here. Being open means we tell you what we’re building, what we won’t do (ads, selling family data), and what’s still unfinished. You deserve that honesty.

§ 02

Promises we keep in public

  • No ads and no ad SDKs in the app.
  • No selling family data for targeting.
  • Privacy-first analytics on the website (Simple Analytics, cookieless).
  • Educational, not medical advice — clinicians help us; the app doesn’t diagnose.
  • Self-funded for now — we take help (e.g. builder credits) without selling out parents.

§ 03

Who builds it

Built by Gl0deanR @ Globinary, with a wife who is a neonatal doctor, another neonatologist friend, and two pediatricians advising on content and safety tone.

Product contact: contact@globinary.io

§ 04

How we build

  • Mobile: Flutter (one codebase for Android & iOS)
  • Offline-first: local SQLite/Drift + sync when signed in
  • API: FastAPI on our VPS · api.bloomdue.baby
  • Landing: static site · this page
  • Auth: email magic codes (no passwords)

§ 05

Numbers we can share today

We’re still tiny. We won’t invent vanity metrics. When we have stable public numbers (beta families, waitlist, store installs), they’ll live here.

  • Business model: free private beta · self-funded
  • Revenue: €0 (by design right now)
  • Access: invite / waitlist via the site form
  • Stores: not published yet (DUNS in progress)
  • Website analytics: privacy-friendly aggregates only

§ 06

Product roadmap

High-level public roadmap — details live on /roadmap.

  1. Now · Private beta Care logs, pregnancy tools, partner sync, Learn cards, calm landing & waitlist.
  2. Next · Polish & trust Store builds, in-app Android APK updates for sideload testers, account isolation, content physician review, pull-to-refresh and partner UX.
  3. Then · Depth Growth charts, preemie/corrected age, richer triage, Romanian i18n.
  4. Later · Toddler & public launch Solids, potty notes, Play Store + App Store for everyone who needs calm tools.
