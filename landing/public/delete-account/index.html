Delete your Enfold account

You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time, from inside the app or by email if you no longer have the app.

§ 01

Delete in the app

  1. Open Enfold and go to Settings.
  2. Open Account & sync.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you only want to stop syncing but keep your logs on the phone, use Sign out instead.

§ 02

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email support@enfold.baby from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Enfold account. We will confirm that the request came from your address, delete the account, and reply when it is done, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

§ 03

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and caregiver name.
  • Your push notification token.
  • If you are the last member of your family in Enfold: the family, the child profiles and every care log (feeds, sleep, diapers, medications, pumping, tummy time, growth, milestones) stored on our server.
  • If a partner remains in the family: the family and its care record stay with them, and your name is no longer linked to an account.

Server backups that may still contain your data are overwritten within a short period, as described in our Privacy Policy. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

§ 04

Delete some of your data

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove something. In the app, open any log in Logs and choose Delete; when you are signed in, the deletion syncs to our server and to your partner's phone. To remove a child profile, your caregiver name or a set of logs without the app, email support@enfold.baby from your sign-up address and say what to remove. We reply when it is done, normally within a few days and always within 30 days.

§ 05

Using Enfold without an account

An account is optional. Without one, Enfold keeps all your logs on your phone and nothing is sent to our server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app removes that data.

§ 06

Contact

Questions about deletion or your data: support@enfold.baby.
