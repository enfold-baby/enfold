# Trademark and brand policy

"Enfold", "Enfold.baby", the Enfold logo, the moon and leaf marks, and the app illustrations
are trademarks and brand assets of Raul Glodean. The AGPL license on the code does not grant
any right to use them. Section 7(e) of the GPL family lets us decline that grant, and we do.

## You may

- Say that your project is "based on Enfold" or "a fork of Enfold", with a link to this repo.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, talks, and academic work to refer to this project.
- Build the unmodified app from source for yourself, your family or your patients.

## You may not

- Publish an app called Enfold, or any name confusingly similar, on Google Play, the App
  Store, F-Droid or anywhere else. Only the official builds from this project carry the name.
- Use the Enfold logo, icon, screenshots, illustrations or the enfold.baby domain in a
  modified version or in a way that suggests we made, endorsed or reviewed it.
- Present a modified version as if it contained clinician-reviewed content from Enfold.
  The learn cards are licensed separately (see `content/LICENSE.md`) and their reviewer
  attributions must not be kept in edited text.

## If you fork

Following the example of [Bluesky's fork guidance](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/blob/main/ASSETS.md):
you have our blessing to fork. Before you distribute, replace the app name, bundle
identifiers (`baby.enfold.app`), app icons, launch images, illustrations, the support and
privacy URLs, and the API base URL. Point your build at your own backend. Do not send your
users' data to `api.enfold.baby`.

## Reporting misuse

If you see an app on a store that copies Enfold's name, icon or content, email
support@enfold.baby. We file takedowns through Google Play's impersonation policy and
Apple's App Store guideline 4.1 (Copycats).
